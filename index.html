The Curriculum

Eight Modules.
One Outcome: Your First Term Sheet.

Each module maps to a critical friction point in the global fundraising journey. Build the narrative, the model, and the confidence — in sequence.

Program Format
12 weeks online + 2 expeditions
10–15 founders per cohort
Weekly live sessions + 1:1 mentorship
English + Chinese bilingual
  • VC decision models: why they invest, why they pass
  • Global vs. regional capital market structural differences
  • How to find the right investor, not just any investor
  • Global competitor scanning and differentiation frameworks
  • TAM/SAM/SOM: correct calculation and investor-ready presentation
  • Translating technical moats into investor language
  • Converting deep tech products into investable business models
  • Valuation logic for SaaS, deep tech, and hardware
  • Revenue model design and unit economics
  • Selecting your first overseas market and entry timing
  • Channel strategy: direct sales vs. partners vs. PLG
  • Common mistakes going global from Taiwan
  • Investor-grade story arc: problem → solution → market → traction → team
  • 10-minute to 2-minute pitch: versions for every context
  • Live practice: real feedback, not polite encouragement
  • What numbers VCs actually look for in a deck
  • 3-year financial projection logic and common mistakes
  • Raise size, valuation, dilution: calculation and negotiation
  • Real impact of liquidation preference, anti-dilution, and pro-rata
  • Which terms are negotiable and which are VC non-negotiables
  • From term sheet to closing: timeline and pitfall avoidance
  • Board management and long-term investor relations
  • Deploying first capital effectively: prioritization framework
  • Signal-building for Series A: KPI design from seed onwards

FAQ

Questions Answered.

Designed for Taiwan-based deep tech founders with a product or early traction who are planning — or have started — to approach global VCs. You don't need to be actively fundraising, but you need to be seriously considering it.
The core curriculum is 12 weeks of online learning with one live session per week. The Ecosystem Expeditions are scheduled separately and run approximately 5–7 days each.
Instruction is primarily in Chinese with bilingual materials. Pitch practice and the Ecosystem Expeditions are conducted in English — because that's the real-world context you're preparing for.
Maximum 15 founders per cohort, intentionally small. Deep peer exchange requires trust, and trust requires time and space — which scale destroys.
Pricing varies based on founder profile and cohort structure. Details are provided after your application is reviewed. GVA's model is not primarily tuition-based — we're more interested in long-term relationships with founders.
Most accelerators help you scale after you raise. We prepare you before you raise. The focus is singular: can you tell a story that global VCs understand and want to follow? That gap determines whether you get into the next conversation.
