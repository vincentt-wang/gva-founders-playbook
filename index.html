FAQ

Questions Answered.

Designed for Taiwan-based deep tech founders with a product or early traction who are planning — or have started — to approach global VCs. You don't need to be actively fundraising, but you need to be seriously considering it.
The core curriculum is 12 weeks of online learning with one live session per week. The Ecosystem Expeditions are scheduled separately and run approximately 5–7 days each.
Instruction is primarily in Chinese with bilingual materials. Pitch practice and the Ecosystem Expeditions are conducted in English — because that's the real-world context you're preparing for.
Maximum 15 founders per cohort, intentionally small. Deep peer exchange requires trust, and trust requires time and space — which scale destroys.
Pricing varies based on founder profile and cohort structure. Details are provided after your application is reviewed. GVA's model is not primarily tuition-based — we're more interested in long-term relationships with founders.
Most accelerators help you scale after you raise. We prepare you before you raise. The focus is singular: can you tell a story that global VCs understand and want to follow? That gap determines whether you get into the next conversation.
